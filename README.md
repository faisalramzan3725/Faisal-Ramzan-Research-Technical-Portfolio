## 🧑‍🔬 Research Profile
AI researcher and industrial PhD candidate specializing in **large language models**, **natural language processing**, **knowledge graphs**, **document intelligence**, and **trustworthy AI**. My research combines lightweight generative models with structured and deterministic methods to build **reliable, scalable, and auditable AI systems**. My experience spans parameter-efficient LLM fine-tuning, semantic technologies, multimodal document understanding, scholarly knowledge systems, and large-scale empirical evaluation, across academic and industrial research in 🇮🇹 Italy and 🇬🇧 the United Kingdom.

**Research interests:** Large Language Models · Retrieval-Augmented Generation · Knowledge Graphs · Document Intelligence · Trustworthy AI · AI in Education
